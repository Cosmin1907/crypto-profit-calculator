# How to include trading and network fees in profit calculations

To accurately include trading and network fees in crypto profit calculations, the key is to add these fees into your cost basis and subtract them from your proceeds to get the true net profit.

Here are the main steps:

- Add all trading fees (brokerage, exchange commissions, spreads) and network fees (also called gas fees for blockchain transactions) paid during buying to the purchase price of your crypto. This increases your effective cost basis.
- Similarly, subtract any fees paid when selling from your gross sale proceeds. This reduces the amount you actually receive.
- The net profit calculation becomes:

$$
\text{Net Profit} = (\text{Sell Price} \times \text{Quantity} - \text{Sell Fees}) - (\text{Buy Price} \times \text{Quantity} + \text{Buy Fees})
$$
- If fees are paid in the cryptocurrency itself instead of fiat, adjust your holdings and cost basis accordingly by valuing the crypto fee at the time of payment.
- Consider all transaction-related costs such as bridge fees when moving assets across blockchains in decentralized finance (DeFi).
- For margin or leveraged trades, also factor in borrowing costs or interest fees to your total expenses.
- Accurately accounting for these fees prevents overestimating profits, giving you a clearer picture of your actual returns and helping with tax calculations.

In sum, always treat fees as part of your investment costs to subtract from gains. This approach ensures your profit reflects all trading and network costs involved.[^1][^2][^3][^5][^6]
