# [how do you calculate crypto profit](https://www.ryrob.com/blog-title-generator/?keyword=how%20do%20you%20calculate%20crypto%20profit&utm_source=keyword-tool)?

To calculate crypto profit, you typically subtract the total purchase cost (including fees) from the total sale proceeds (also after fees). The basic formula is:

- Profit = Selling Price - Purchase Price

For a percentage profit, divide the profit by the purchase price and multiply by 100:

- Percentage Profit = (Profit / Purchase Price) × 100

If you hold cryptocurrency without selling, unrealized gains can be calculated by subtracting the initial investment from the current market value.

Additional factors like transaction fees must be included on both buy and sell sides to get the net profit. Some advanced calculations like annualized returns consider the holding period to compare investment performance over time.

Key points for calculating crypto profit:

- Account for all transaction fees on buying and selling
- Use the formula: Net Profit = (Sell Price × Amount) - (Buy Price × Amount) - Total Fees
- Percentage gain shows the profitability relative to investment
- Consider realized profits (from sales) versus unrealized profits (current holdings)
- Tools and calculators are available online to simplify these calculations

This approach ensures precise profit evaluation in crypto trading or investing.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
