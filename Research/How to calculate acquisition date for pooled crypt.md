# How to calculate acquisition date for pooled crypto holdings

For pooled crypto holdings, the acquisition date is generally calculated using a pooling method such as the Section 104 pool (UK tax rules) or similar average cost basis methods:

- When you acquire identical cryptocurrencies at different times and prices, they form a "pool" with an average cost basis.
- The acquisition date for the pool is effectively averaged or treated as the date the holdings were pooled, but important matching rules apply.
- Specific matching rules apply first:
    - Same-day rule: Match disposals with acquisitions on the same day.
    - 30-day rule: Match disposals with acquisitions within the next 30 days.
- After applying these rules, the remaining holdings go into the pool with a calculated average acquisition cost and date.
- To calculate the average acquisition cost per unit, divide the total purchase cost of all pooled assets by the total number of assets in the pool.
- Use this average cost basis and pooled acquisition date for calculating gains or losses on disposals.
- Maintaining detailed records of all acquisitions and disposals is necessary to correctly apply these rules.

In essence, acquisition date for pooled crypto holdings is computed considering same-day and 30-day acquisition matches first, then applying an average cost and generalized date for remaining pooled assets.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
