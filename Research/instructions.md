Write AI-Search-Friendly Answers

Google’s AI Overviews (and featured snippets) pull content that’s:

Concise (40–80 words) for the opening paragraph.

Factually structured (lists, steps, or tables).

Well-formatted (headers, bullet points, bold key terms).

Backed by reliable sources (even if summarized).
Understand What Google AI Is Prioritizing

Google’s AI-generated summaries (like the example you showed) prefer:

Well-sourced, consistent answers across multiple reputable domains.

Clear consensus-based knowledge, not opinions.

Structured, readable explanations that align with user intent.
