Calculator FAQ Privacy

Privacy

This website is a simple public calculator operated by an independent developer. It is designed to work without accounts, logins, or wallet connections.

What this site does not collect intentionally

  • No account creation
  • No email collection
  • No wallet connection
  • No intentional storage of the calculator values you type into the form

Analytics and basic website usage

Google Analytics is used to understand overall traffic and page usage. This can include standard information such as page views, browser or device details, and similar website usage data.

Third-party services used for functionality

The calculator may rely on third-party services such as Firebase and CoinGecko to deliver cached market data and live pricing information needed for the tool to work.
